# Ramu Voice Assistant - Supported Commands

## 📱 App Control

### Open Apps
- "Open WhatsApp"
- "Launch Chrome"
- "Start YouTube"
- "Open Gmail"
- "Launch Maps"
- "Open Camera"

## 📞 Phone & Messaging

### Make Calls
- "Call John"
- "Phone Sarah"
- "Dial Mike"
- "Call Mom"

### Send WhatsApp Messages
- "Send WhatsApp message to John"
- "WhatsApp John saying Hello"
- "Message John on WhatsApp - How are you"
- "Text John on WhatsApp"

### Send SMS
- "Send SMS to Sarah"
- "Text message to Mike"
- "Send message to Mom saying I'll be late"

## 🔧 System Controls

### Bluetooth
- "Turn on Bluetooth"
- "Enable Bluetooth"
- "Switch on Bluetooth"
- "Bluetooth on"
- "Turn off Bluetooth"
- "Disable Bluetooth"
- "Bluetooth off"

### WiFi
- "Turn on WiFi"
- "Enable WiFi"
- "Switch on WiFi"
- "WiFi on"
- "Turn off WiFi"
- "Disable WiFi"
- "WiFi off"

### Camera
- "Open Camera"
- "Launch Camera"
- "Take a photo"
- "Take a picture"
- "Take a selfie"

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Speak clearly** and at a normal pace
2. **Use natural language** - the assistant understands variations
3. **Contact names** should match your phone contacts
4. **For messages**, you can say:
   - "Message John saying Hello"
   - "Text Sarah - How are you"
   - "WhatsApp Mike: See you soon"

## ⚙️ Setup Requirements

Make sure you have granted all permissions:
- ✅ Microphone (for voice recognition)
- ✅ Contacts (for calls and messages)
- ✅ Phone & Bluetooth (for calls and Bluetooth control)
- ✅ Accessibility Service (for WhatsApp automation)

## 🔄 Background Service

Enable the background service to have Ramu always listening:
1. Tap "Start Service" button
2. Ramu will run in the background
3. Speak commands anytime
4. Tap "Stop Service" to disable
